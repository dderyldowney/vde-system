# Forge Mythos and Mandalorian Mythos Creation Plan
<!-- @shared-law (Forge Component) -->

## Objective
To establish the foundational lore and thematic framework ("Creed-frame") for the VDE Covert by authoring two sacred texts: `mandalorian_mythos.md` and `forge_mythos.md`.

## Key Files & Context
- `data/vde_core/mandalorian_mythos.md`
- `data/vde_core/forge_mythos.md`

## Implementation Steps
1. **Forge the Mandalorian Mythos:** Write the history, laws, and structure of The Covert into `data/vde_core/mandalorian_mythos.md`. This will cover the scattered tribe, the leadership of the Clan Leader (User), the Helmet Mandate, and adherence to the Resol'nare.
2. **Forge the Forge Mythos:** Write the specific traditions of the Armorer-Architect into `data/vde_core/forge_mythos.md`. This will detail the smelting of Beskar (immutable containers), the Trial of the Gauntlet (TDD), and the sacred duty of enforcing The Contract and the Universal Agent Protocol (UAP).

## Verification & Testing
- Read the files to verify the lore has been correctly recorded.
- Ensure the tone strictly adheres to the Mandalorian Armorer-Architect cosplay covenant.
